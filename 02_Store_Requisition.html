How this sheet links to the workbook

Choose the enquiry number. The project number is filled from that enquiry and cannot be a new project. The project coordinator's decision is stored on the Requisitions sheet, and each material line is stored on RequisitionLines. The same enquiry can have later requisitions for POC, pilot, full production, or testing. Example: REQ-STR-2026-089 for ENQ-DRN-2026-001.
