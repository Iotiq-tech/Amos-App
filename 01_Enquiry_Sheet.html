How this sheet links to the workbook

Saving this form writes one row on the Enquiries sheet and creates the project number. Store requisitions, outside test passes, POC, pilot, full production, and testing all keep that same project number. Pick lists for people, drone types, electronics, budgets, and locations come from the master workbook. The worked example is ENQ-DRN-2026-001 / PRJ-DRN-2026-001.
